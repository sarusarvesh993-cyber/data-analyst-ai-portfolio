# Stakeholder brief — E-commerce Revenue & Cohort Analysis

## What the data says

The marketplace recorded **96,478 delivered orders** and **R$13.22 million in item GMV** from September 2016 through August 2018. Average delivered-order GMV was **R$137.04**, but only **3.0% of customers** placed at least two delivered orders during the observation window.

Delivery reliability is strongly associated with customer experience. On-time deliveries averaged **4.29 stars**, compared with **2.57 stars** for late deliveries. Low reviews (one or two stars) occurred on **54.0% of late deliveries** versus **9.2% of on-time deliveries**.

November 2017 produced the highest complete-month GMV at **R$987.8K**, while its on-time delivery rate fell to **85.7%**. Health and beauty led categories with **R$1.23M** in delivered-order GMV.

## Recommended actions

1. **Build a repeat-purchase program:** instrument lifecycle campaigns by first category and test a second-order offer against a holdout. The 3.0% repeat-customer rate is a measurement and growth priority, not proof that promotions will work.
2. **Prioritize delivery reliability:** investigate high-volume states and categories below the portfolio's 91.9% on-time rate. Separate carrier, seller, distance, and promise-setting causes before selecting an intervention.
3. **Protect peak-month experience:** add capacity and delivery-risk monitoring before demand spikes. November 2017 combined the highest monthly GMV with weaker on-time performance and lower reviews.

## Important limitations

- Item GMV is transaction value, not Olist revenue or profit.
- The historical observation window ends in 2018.
- Customer repeat behavior may be understated by the available identity and time window.
- The delivery-review relationship is descriptive and does not prove causation.
